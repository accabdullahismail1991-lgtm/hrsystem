🏁 End of Service Gratuity / مكافأة نهاية الخدمة
KSA Labor Law — Article 84 & 87 | نظام العمل السعودي — المادتان 84 و87
📋 KSA Labor Law Policy Reference / مرجع نظام العمل السعودي
Article 84 — Employee-Resigned / الاستقالة
  • Less than 2 years → No entitlement / لا يستحق
  • 2–5 years → ⅓ of 10-day wage per year
  • 5–10 years → ⅔ of 10-day wage per year
  • 10+ years → Full 10-day wage per year
Article 87 — Employer-Terminated / الفصل أو انتهاء العقد
  • First 5 years → Half-month salary per year
  • After 5 years → One full month salary per year
  • Partial year → Prorated by months served / بالأشهر
  • Basis: Last basic salary + housing allowance
